## Rules for the 100%

As stated in [speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/fr-FR/syphon_filter_2?h=100&rules=game&x=zd3le0vd), here are the rules for the 100% :

- unlock every multiplayer stages
- unlock every multiplayer characters
- unlock every bonus sequences
- open every weapon boxes in the game (not the ones containing flak jackets)

Valkyrie_ZB has provided a [document](https://www.speedrun.com/fr-FR/syphon_filter_2/resources/jixcf) for the 100%.

⚠️  Important note : In this document, the supply truck in the level 3 is not registered as a weapon box (the truck with grenades too) but the police car with TGL in level 12 is registered. So, I wanted to mention that I've just followed this document to reach what is defined as 100%.

To unlock bonus sequences, the game has to be played in hard difficulty.

When Gabe / Lian says "Got it" during the game then a secret is unlocked.

To see the currently unlocked secrets, pause the game and go to `Options` then `Cheats`.

### Hard difficulty

#### Unlock hard difficulty

The hard difficulty is unlocked by holding Up + L1 + Select + Square + Circle + X + R2 at the title screen (a "ding" sound is played).

#### Differences between normal and hard difficulty

In a dedicated [gamefaqs' french page](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/198877-syphon-filter-2/faqs/49529), differences are listed for the hard difficulty :

- More enemies
- Enemies hide more and target better
- More enemies will target your head
- During manual aim (L1), headshots are no more indicated
- Enemies take sometimes other routes when they patrol (on stealthy missions)

Differences by level are listed too, but I just want to highlight that yes some enemies are better shooters and so, some strats are possible in normal difficulty but may not be possible in hard difficulty.

### Tricks used in many levels

#### ☑️ Fast weapon box opening

If Gabe / Lian crouches during nearly 18 frames before opening a box, it reduces the time (by nearly 36 frames) of the animation (removing the first part which is putting one knee on the ground).

If Gabe / Lian has a high fire rate weapon (H-11, G-18, M-16, ...), a box can be opened in less frames by opening it "between" two consecutive shots.

#### ☑️ Knee on the ground animation skip

When Gabe / Lian takes a radio call, he may put a knee on the ground. It can be skipped by crouching or rolling just before.

#### ☑️ Fast raise on feet

Just after a radio call, if the weapon switches from a two-hands one (sniper, M-16, ...) to a one-hand one (grenade, G-18, ...) the raise on feet will be quicker.

#### ✅ Restart at last checkpoint

To avoid some backtracking, the "Restart at last checkpoint" option can be used.

#### ☑️ / ✅ No stun after fall

Gabe / Lian can avoid being stunned after a medium fall by doing a roll just before falling.

If the fall is higher, the stun can be avoided by crouching in the air after the roll.

If the fall is high enough to die when touching the ground, try to find a ledge to grab on the way (cf level 9) or something to collide with (cf level 12).

#### ☑️ Manual aim during climbing

This trick has been [explained by BaronHaynes](https://www.speedrun.com/fr-FR/syphon_filter_2/forums/rt8n8).

#### ☑️ / ✅ Respawn skip

After cutscenes, Gabe / Lian will be relocated at specific coordinates. This relocation can be avoided by grabbing a ledge at the beginning of the cutscene.

#### ☑️ Aiming during a U-turn

When aiming with L1 (not R1), instead of turning round to get a good angle to shoot, make a U-turn and aim during it. It may be a TAS-only move, but it's quite faster.

#### ✅ Fast strafing

Instead of keeping left or right always pushed during a strafe, keep this pace : direction pushed during ~ 21 frames then released during ~ 3 frames. It will be a faster strafe (cf level 15 & 20).

### Level 1

At 1:13, cf `Restart at last checkpoint`.

#### ☑️ Cutscene skip

At 1:24, you can avoid talking to this GI but, if you do, you would be able to skip a cutscene by being ahead of the two GIs until after the first tunnel and quickly rolling out the path (at 01:47) or the second cutscene will play there.

#### ☑️ Ledge grab skip

At 2:57, you can avoid a ledge grab when going out the tunnel with a quite precise roll.

### Level 2

#### ✅ Discussion skip (n°1)

At 8:10, you can get your adrenaline shot without waiting for the discussion between Elsa Weisinger and the guard to finish by crouch-forwarding near Elsa between "Doctor !" and "Yes ?".

A less hardcore move is to despawn them by going in front of the A7 door ([example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xHCbCzChzlM&t=428s)).

#### ✅ Switch skip (n°1)

At 8:27, to skip the switch (and its cutscene) leading to Lian's gear, a roll has to be made to bypass the guard who is watching the corner.

#### ✅ Discussion skip (n°2)

At 8:48, to skip the discussion between Carter and the army GI, you roll from the army GI to the wall when their discussion begins.

#### ✅ Switch skip (n°2)

At 9:08, after getting her gear, Lian can skip the switch near Carter by hand tasing the army GI (who wants to get some air) close to the door.

### Level 3

At 12:04, cf `Respawn skip`.

#### ✅ Supply truck skip

If you take care of the guards near Chance right away at the beginning of the level, you won't need to go to the supply truck first then backtrack to Chance.

#### ☑️ Knife kill animation skip

At 9:58, just before the knife cut, a crouch is done. This allows to skip the knife kill animation.

### Level 4

#### ✅ Explosives' path shortcut

At 14:24, roll from explosive 2 to 3 without going up to the bridge.

### Level 5

#### 🌟 Glitchy alert state

At 18:38, Lian is exiting the place when the army GI is in an alert state (because he has found the body of the agency pilot) and she's despawning him from the radar (if you exit before that, the mission fails because the alarm is triggered), this is the cause of a glitchy alert state. At that point, the mission can't fail because of being seen by the army GIs (but will fail if you are seen by Holman for example). It offers a couple of sequence breaks where you don't have to find some workarounds to avoid being seen by army GIs, it's very satisfactory. I've seen this glitch description in the comments section of L&K's run.

### Level 6 & 7

#### ✅ Stun animations skip

When performing long jumps, if Gabe is landing further down then sometimes a stun animation is played. But if a grenade is exploding just before Gabe's landing then this stun animation is skipped. It has been made many times during the two train levels. Additionally, with some rng manipulation, the long jumps without grenades can have their stun animations skipped too.

### Level 9

#### ☑️ Discussion skip (part of)

At 30:55, army GIs' discussion is shortened by crossbowing the one next to the door (but not too soon).

#### ✅ The "Pharcom shortcut"

At 32:30, by being quite precisely placed, this roll will allow to not go to the end of the alley and then go down from there (cf `No stun after fall`).

#### ☑️ Cutscene skip

At 33:05, throwing the grenade and waiting allows to skip the cutscene where two enemies are talking.

### Level 10

At 36:11, 37:15 and 38:03, cf `Respawn skip`.

### Level 12

#### ✅ Street shortcut

At 44:32, it seems that touching something (here the lamp) during a too high fall resets the height where you begin to fall, allowing to not dying from it (cf `No stun after fall`). This shortcut skips a long jump and do not trigger some guard arrivals.

### Level 15

Note : In L&K's run, the crossbow can be used (because it's an Any% not a 100%) so it's much more quicker (than just using the hand taser). This is the reason why the sequence break at 47m43 (in their video) is possible but not in this TAS.

At 50:58, cf `Fast strafing`.

### Level 16

At 56:29, cf `Respawn skip`.

### Level 17

Note : At 1:00:13, it's not possible to skip the knife kill animation here because Gabe (in this level) won't crouch when pressing X.

#### 🌟 Gherson (long walk) skip

At 1:00:46, instead of killing the two guards (to then talk with Ramirez), take care of just the guard further down, go to Gherson and then kill the second guard. It will skip the long walk of Gherson.

At 1:02:38, the lab guy in his control room can be shot in his stomach, skipping the switch to push on.

### Level 18

At 1:07:14, pressing down when quitting the upper vent will push you forward (seems like the u-turn action gives you quite some speed). It skips a cutscene and cancels some full-armored guards arrival (so it saves 5 M-79 ammunition for the NY Slums level).

### Level 19

#### ☑️ Theresa call skip

At 1:09:40, by grabbing the washing machine and move a little to the right, this will skip a call from Theresa.

### Level 20

At 1:14:22, cf `Fast strafing`.

#### 🌟 Triple cutscene skip

At 1:14:57, by doing this right angle, it will skip 3 cutscenes with Theresa.
